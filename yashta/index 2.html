What is Jyotish?

Jyotish is the traditional astrological system of India. It looks at the chart as a map of karma, timing, relationship, vocation, and the deeper movement of life.

Rather than reducing astrology to personality, Jyotish helps reveal the patterns shaping experience and the timing through which those patterns unfold.

Pattern recognition

A reading can help clarify recurring themes, natural strengths, karmic pressures, and the deeper structure of your life path.

Timing and transition

Jyotish offers tools for understanding the timing of change, including major life periods, current pressures, and openings for growth.

Grounded spiritual orientation

Yashta's approach is practical, conversational, and rooted in discernment. The goal is not fatalism, but clearer participation with the life you are living.

Focus

Each session is shaped around your chart, current timing, and the questions you bring.

  • Chart orientation

    Core life themes, strengths, challenges, karmic patterns, and the overall structure of the birth chart.

  • Life timing

    Current cycles, transition periods, decision points, and the larger rhythm of what is unfolding.

  • Relationships

    Compatibility, relational dynamics, emotional patterns, and the karma activated through partnership.

  • Vocation and purpose

    Work, calling, service, livelihood, and the ways your gifts want to move into form.

  • Spiritual development

    Practice, devotion, inner maturation, and the deeper orientation of the soul through the chart.

  • Remedies and integration

    Simple supports for working with planetary patterns through awareness, practice, rhythm, and refinement.
